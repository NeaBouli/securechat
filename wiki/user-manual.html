Overview

What Is SecureChat?

SecureChat is an end-to-end encrypted messenger where no message ever touches a server in readable form. Messages are encrypted on your device before they leave it, using XChaCha20-Poly1305 with a Double Ratchet for forward secrecy. There is no phone number, no email address, no cloud account, and no registration. Your identity is a cryptographic key pair generated locally and stored only on your device.

Because there is no relay server for messages, both users need to be online simultaneously for delivery — or use the built-in QR export/import method for fully offline, air-gapped communication.

First-Time Setup

Step 1 — Identity Creation

On first launch, SecureChat silently generates your cryptographic identity: an Ed25519 signing key and an X25519 encryption key. This happens before any UI is shown. Your keys are stored locally only — they are never sent to any server and do not leave your device.

Step 2 — Biometric Unlock

SecureChat uses biometric authentication (fingerprint, face, or device PIN) to protect access. A biometric prompt appears on every launch. Authenticate to proceed. If biometric hardware is unavailable, the app opens directly.

Recommendation: Keep biometric unlock enabled at all times. It is the first line of defense against unauthorized access to your messages.

Step 3 — Add Your First Contact

The conversations screen will be empty on first launch. Tap the + button (bottom right) to open the Add Contact screen. You cannot send messages without first adding a contact and exchanging public keys.

Navigation

Main Screen (Conversations)

The home screen shows all active conversations sorted by last message time. Unread messages appear with a red badge. Tap a conversation to open it.

The toolbar has three controls:

  • ID button (left) — opens your My ID screen
  • Lock icon (center) — your 5-tap emergency wipe trigger (see below)
  • Settings (right) — opens Settings
Adding Contacts

How to Add a Contact

Tap + on the home screen to open the Add Contact screen. If you are at the contact limit (Free tier: 10), a red card appears and the input field is disabled. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited contacts.

Method 1 — Scan QR Code

Tap Scan QR Code. The camera opens. Point it at your contact’s QR code, shown on their My ID screen. The app validates the Ed25519 signature automatically and adds the contact.

Method 2 — Paste QR Content

If your contact cannot show their QR in person, they can share the text content of their QR (via any messenger, email, or printed). Paste it into the input field. The field expects the format stealthx://add/sx_...?.... Tap Add Contact when the button becomes active.

Method 3 — NFC

NFC-based contact exchange is coming in a future update.

Messaging

Sending a Message

Open a conversation and type in the text field at the bottom. Tap Send. Each message is encrypted individually before leaving your device. Message status indicators:

  • Pending — queued for delivery
  • Sent — delivered to recipient’s device
  • Failed — delivery failed (recipient likely offline)

QR Message Export (Air-Gapped)

Tap the QR button in the bottom bar to export your last outgoing message as a QR code. Show this QR to your contact — they scan it using the QR scanner icon in their chat’s top bar. This works completely offline with no internet on either side.

Importing a Message

Tap the file icon in the top bar to open the import field. Paste the text content of a message received outside the app. This is the text equivalent of the QR export.

Safety Number Verification

Tap the Shield icon in the top bar to see the Safety Number for this conversation. A Safety Number is a series of digit groups derived from your shared secret. Contact your conversation partner via a separate channel and compare the numbers out loud. If they match, your keys are genuine — no man-in-the-middle attack has occurred.

Your Identity

My ID Screen

Tap the ID button (top left of home screen) to see your identity.

  • StealthX ID — your unique identifier, format: sx_ followed by 9 characters (e.g. sx_a7Kx9mPq2)
  • QR code — encodes your full public key bundle (Ed25519 + X25519 keys with signature)
  • Custom handle — shown above your SX_ID if set
  • Share button — sends the text version of your ID via the system share sheet
Note: Your StealthX ID works for both SecureChat and SecureCall. Share it once — your contact can reach you on both apps.
Settings Reference

Tier Status Card

At the top of Settings, a card shows your current tier badge (FREE / PRO / ELITE), your locked IFR balance if verified, your wallet address if connected, and the cache status (30-day window).

Security

Biometric Unlock

When enabled, the app requires biometric authentication (fingerprint, face, or PIN) every time it is opened or resumed from the background. Default: On. Only disable if you have another device-level lock in place.

Stealth Delete (5-tap)

When enabled, tapping the lock icon in the Conversations toolbar five times triggers an immediate, irreversible wipe. Default: On. What is wiped:

  • The entire encrypted message database (including WAL and SHM journal files)
  • All encrypted preferences (biometric settings, IFR wallet data)
  • Your StealthX identity (keys)
  • All app cache and code cache directories
Warning: The wipe is immediate and irreversible. There is no backup and no recovery. After the wipe the app closes.

Free Features

  • E2E Encrypted Messaging — XChaCha20-Poly1305 + Double Ratchet. Available to all tiers.
  • QR Key Exchange — Device-to-device key exchange via QR code or text. No server involved. Available to all tiers.
  • Contact Limit — Shows your current usage. Free: 10 max. Pro and Elite: unlimited.

Pro Features ≥ 2,000 IFR

Each of the following shows a lock icon and Unlock button if your tier is below Pro.

  • Group Messaging — Encrypted group chats.
  • Encrypted File Transfer — End-to-end encrypted file sharing via Kaspa XFTP.
  • Kaspa Identity Anchor — Publish your public key to the Kaspa BlockDAG for permanent, verifiable identity anchoring.
  • Chameleon Integration — Connects SecureChat to the Chameleon Privacy OS as its encrypted messaging backend.

Elite Features ≥ 6,000 IFR

Each of the following shows a lock icon and Unlock button if your tier is below Elite.

  • Onion Routing (3-hop) — Routes all traffic through a three-hop anonymization network, hiding your IP from every node.
  • Decoy Chat Profiles — Create fake conversation histories that appear when a decoy PIN is entered.
  • Advanced Threat Detection — Real-time behavioral analysis that detects surveillance patterns and alerts you.
  • Emergency Broadcast — Send an encrypted alert message to all contacts simultaneously. Tap to open the Broadcast screen.

IFR Token Unlock

Tap IFR Token Unlock in Settings to open the unlock screen.

Connect Wallet (WalletConnect)

Tapping Connect Wallet launches your installed Ethereum wallet app (MetaMask, Trust Wallet, etc.). Your wallet signs a challenge message proving ownership of the address. SecureChat then queries the IFR token contract directly on Ethereum Mainnet via public RPC endpoints (no account or API key required).

Manual Address Entry

Enter your Ethereum address (0x format) manually. SecureChat verifies the locked balance on-chain. Manual verifications expire after 30 days. The app re-checks every 24 hours while the cache is valid. If re-verification fails (offline), the cached tier is kept until expiry.

Emergency Wipe

5-Tap Wipe

On the Conversations screen, the lock icon in the toolbar center is the wipe trigger. Tap it five times rapidly. There is no confirmation dialog and no delay — all data is destroyed immediately and the app closes.

This is designed for high-urgency situations where you need to destroy the app’s contents faster than you can navigate to a settings menu.

Troubleshooting

Common Issues

  • Contact is not receiving my messages — Both users must be online simultaneously for delivery. If your contact is offline, use QR message export to deliver the message without an internet connection.
  • Scanning QR did not add the contact — Ensure you are scanning from the contact’s My ID screen directly (FLAG_SECURE may block screenshots). Ask them to share the text version of their ID instead.
  • Biometric fails and the app closes — The app closes on biometric failure to protect your data. Check device biometric enrollment in Android Settings.
  • Tier shows Free after IFR verification — Ensure your tokens are locked in the IFR contract, not just held in your wallet. Visit ifrunit.tech to lock tokens. Then return to Settings → IFR Token Unlock and verify again.
  • Wiped by accident — The wipe is irreversible by design. There is no backup and no recovery. Your contacts will need to re-add you using your new identity after reinstall.
